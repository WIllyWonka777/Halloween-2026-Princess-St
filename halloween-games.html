🎃 Pumpkin Carving

No sign-up needed — just show up at 5:30 PM and grab a spot. Carving runs until 9PM when the night kicks into full gear.
🥃 Prize: Best pumpkin wins a tequila bottle — judged at 10PM by the crowd 🏆
WHAT WE PROVIDE
🎃
10 Pumpkins
First come, first served — or bring your own if you want a specific size.
🔪
Carving Tools
Scoops, saws, detail blades — enough for everyone. Bring your own if you have a favourite.
📄
Stencil Cutouts
Classic and spooky patterns to trace. Or skip them entirely — freestyle gets extra respect.
⚠️ ONE RULE
Clean up your station when you're done — guts, seeds, paper scraps. Bags and wipes will be on the table. This is a shared space and everyone appreciates it.
